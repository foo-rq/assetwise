Changes to consider

How these suggestions are calculated

We start with 15% and 30% asset allocations and show up to 3 changes. We also test adding cash to each mix when needed to meet your loss screens, so final percentages may be smaller. Other positions scale proportionally; protected cash stays available. Your period sets the growth comparison. Your loss limit checks past drawdown and modelled losses along the way. Future losses can exceed both. Volatility must fall by 0.1 percentage points, except when a smaller reduction meets your loss limit. Growth and preservation use different rankings. Keeping your portfolio is also an option.

Analysis limits

Historical prices include provider adjustments for splits and distributions. Future returns are estimates. Weights rebalance every session. Cash earns 0%; costs and taxes are excluded. Return is the arithmetic mean multiplied by 252; volatility uses the square root of 252. Maximum drawdown is the largest decline from a previous peak. Sharpe uses monthly Treasury-bill returns converted to daily equivalents. Forecasts use only data through the displayed decision date. Dated fund holdings have partial coverage, shown in the portfolio report.
